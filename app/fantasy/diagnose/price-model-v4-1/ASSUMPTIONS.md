# Assumptions

- The 2026/27 team budget remains 100m.
- The 2025/26 matched player pool is a reasonable market-price anchor.
- A maximum 3% increase in average price is a provisional guardrail, not a learned historical parameter.
- Final 19Fantasy prices remain bounded to 1–20m and use 0.5m steps.

The 3% guardrail should be re-estimated if we recover full historical player-price distributions for more seasons.
