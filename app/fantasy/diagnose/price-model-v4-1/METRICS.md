# Key metrics

The top cards are intended to make model drift obvious:

- 25/26 average price
- raw 26/27 average
- calibrated 26/27 average
- applied market scale
- number of players moving at least +3m
- number of players moving at most -3m

A raw average materially above the calibrated average means the individual model is producing market-wide inflation and the guardrail is actively correcting it.
