# Interpretation

The `budgetSharePct` column is intentionally simple and directly actionable: with a 100m roster budget, a 12m player consumes 12% of the available budget.

The `marketSharePct` column is different: it measures the player's share of the total value of the analysed player pool. It is useful for checking whether value is becoming too concentrated at the top end.

The two measures should not be conflated.
